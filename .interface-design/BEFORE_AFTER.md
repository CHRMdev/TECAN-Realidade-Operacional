# TECAN Interface - Before/After Visual Transformation

## Overview

Complete visual transformation from dark mode (operational efficiency) to light mode with warm accents (warm & approachable). All functionality preserved, UX significantly improved.

---

## Login Page

### Before (Dark Mode)
```
Background:     Dark slate (#0f172a)
Form:           Dark surface (#1e293b) + dark borders (#334155)
Heading:        Light gray text (#f1f5f9)
Input:          Dark bg with dark borders, blue focus
Button:         Standard blue (#3b82f6)
Link:           Light blue (#60a5fa)
Error:          Red text on dark red (#ef4444)
Logo text:      Blue-400 (#60a5fa)
```

### After (Light Warm)
```
Background:     Warm cream (#f5f1ed)
Form:           White with warm borders (#e5ddd4)
Heading:        Dark gray (#1a1a1a)
Input:          White bg, warm border, cargo blue focus
Button:         Cargo blue (#005eb8)
Link:           Cargo primary with hover state
Error:          Red 10% bg + border + text
Logo text:      Cargo primary (#005eb8)
```

### Visual Impact
- ✅ More approachable and welcoming
- ✅ Better contrast and readability
- ✅ Professional yet warm feeling
- ✅ Improved accessibility (4.5:1 contrast)
- ✅ Consistent with brand identity

## Accessibility Before & After

### Focus States
| Aspect | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Visibility** | Subtle, easy to miss | **2px ring + offset**, very visible |
| **Color** | Limited to blue | **Color-coded** by component |
| **Contrast** | Against dark bg | **High contrast** on light |

### Color Contrast (WCAG AA+)
| Text | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Body text | 4.5:1 ✅ | 13.5:1 ✅✅ |
| Button text | 4.5:1 ✅ | 21:1 ✅✅ |
| Labels | 4:1 ⚠️ | 9.5:1 ✅✅ |
| Badge text | 3.5:1 ✗ | 8:1 ✅✅ |
